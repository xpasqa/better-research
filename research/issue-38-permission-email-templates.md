# Issue #38 — Template Permintaan Izin Instrumen

Template ini disimpan di repository agar permintaan izin dapat dikirim secara konsisten setelah peneliti siap menggunakan akun/institusi yang sesuai. Tidak ada email yang dianggap sudah dikirim hanya karena template ini tersedia.

## Template umum

**Subject:** Permission to use and translate [instrument name] for a doctoral study in Indonesia

Dear [Author/Rightsholder],

I am preparing a non-commercial doctoral study involving junior secondary school students in Jakarta, Indonesia. I would like to request permission to use [instrument name] and, where necessary, translate/adapt the items into Bahasa Indonesia while preserving the original construct and scoring structure.

Please let me know whether permission is granted for:

1. research administration;
2. Bahasa Indonesia translation/adaptation; and
3. reproduction of the translated items in a dissertation appendix, or whether the items should remain restricted to survey materials.

The original source will be fully cited and all translation/adaptation procedures will be documented. If there is an official permission form, preferred version, scoring guide, or condition of use, I would be grateful if you could share it.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
[Researcher name]
[Institution / doctoral program]
