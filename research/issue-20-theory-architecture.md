# Issue #20 — Rebuild Theoretical Architecture

## 0. Decision rule

Teori tidak dipilih untuk menghias diagram SEM. Setiap teori harus mempunyai fungsi yang berbeda dan necessary: (1) menjelaskan posisi konteks, (2) menjelaskan proximal motivational/developmental process, atau (3) menyediakan rival explanation. Jika teori tidak menghasilkan prediction/boundary yang dapat dibedakan, teori tersebut tidak ditambahkan.

## 1. Final architecture: two-layer core + explicit competing mechanisms

### Layer A — Neo-ecological positioning

Bronfenbrenner digunakan dalam versi yang mature/qualified, bukan sebagai generic `grand theory`. Rosa & Tudge menunjukkan evolusi dari ecological ke bioecological/PPCT dan pentingnya person–process–context–time [@rosa2013bronfenbrenner]. Navarro & Tudge memperluas ecological reasoning ke virtual/digital microsystems [@navarro2023neoecological].

Fungsi layer ini hanya:

- menempatkan family digital context (DPM) dan school/teacher context (PTS) sebagai proximal contexts yang dialami remaja;
- menegaskan bahwa contexts dapat bekerja secara berbeda, simultan, dan transactional;
- memotivasi perhatian terhadap cross-context reinforcement/compensation dan developmental timing;
- mencegah reduksi adolescent functioning menjadi satu predictor tunggal.

**Yang tidak diklaim:** model Gemma bukan full empirical test of PPCT karena tidak memodelkan semua PPCT components, repeated proximal processes pada micro-time scale, atau complete nested ecological system.

### Layer B — Self-Determination Theory / self-system mechanism for engagement

Untuk menjelaskan mengapa perceived support dapat berkaitan dengan engagement, core proximal theory adalah **Self-Determination Theory (SDT)** dan self-system process logic yang kompatibel dengannya. SDT menyatakan bahwa contexts yang support autonomy, competence, dan relatedness facilitate autonomous motivation and adaptive engagement; need-thwarting contexts cenderung menghasilkan pola sebaliknya [@ryandeci2020sdt].

Meta-analytic evidence memperkuat mechanism ini. Howard et al. mensintesis 8,693 correlations dari 637 samples (N=388,912) dan menemukan need-supportive teacher/parent behaviors berkaitan positif dengan engagement, performance, dan well-being, sementara need-thwarting behaviors menunjukkan pola berlawanan [@howard2025needsupport]. Bureau et al. menemukan dalam meta-analysis >79,000 students bahwa teacher autonomy support lebih kuat terkait need satisfaction/self-determined motivation daripada parental autonomy support, dengan competence need sebagai predictor terkuat dari autonomous motivation [@bureau2022motivation]. Pada early adolescents, Stroet et al. juga menemukan positive association konsisten antara need-supportive teaching (autonomy support, structure, involvement) dan motivation/engagement [@stroet2013needsupport].

Fungsi layer ini:

- memberi proximal explanatory pathway dari **PTS → student motivational resources → engagement**;
- menjelaskan mengapa emotional/instrumental/appraisal/informational support tidak sekadar “baik” secara normatif, tetapi dapat menyediakan resources yang memenuhi kebutuhan autonomy/competence/relatedness;
- menyediakan rival mediator yang jelas: **basic psychological need satisfaction / autonomous motivation**.

## 2. Where SEC fits—and where it does not

SEC **bukan construct SDT**. Karena itu SEC tidak boleh dipresentasikan sebagai mediator yang “diturunkan langsung” dari SDT.

SEC ditempatkan sebagai **developmental regulatory/social capacity mechanism** yang dapat membantu siswa mengubah contextual resources menjadi adaptive participation: self-management mendukung persistence/regulation; social awareness/relationship competence mempermudah use of interpersonal support; responsible decision-making dapat membantu adaptive participation. SEC→engagement mempunyai direct systematic-review support [@santos2023secengagement].

Namun theoretical architecture mengharuskan SEC bersaing dengan lebih proximal motivational mechanisms. Dengan kata lain:

> PTS → SEC → engagement adalah plausible developmental pathway, tetapi SDT memprediksi alternatif PTS → need satisfaction/autonomous motivation → engagement yang mungkin lebih proximal.

Ini menghasilkan testable theoretical tension, bukan theory stacking.

## 3. DPM is not the same as parental support

DPM merupakan context-specific practices untuk mengelola kehidupan digital remaja. Tidak semua DPM dimension adalah need-supportive. Karena itu SDT tidak digunakan untuk membenarkan global `DPM → SEC/engagement` positive path.

### Enabling mediation

Enabling/interpretative mediation dapat bersifat autonomy/competence supportive jika melibatkan explanation, dialogue, collaborative rule-making, dan guidance yang meningkatkan agency/understanding. Maka pathway berikut **theory-consistent tetapi belum established**:

> enabling DPM → autonomy/competence-supportive experience → regulatory/social competence and/or autonomous motivation → engagement.

### Restrictive mediation

Restriction dapat berfungsi berbeda menurut **implementation quality**:

- structured, predictable, rationalized boundaries dapat support competence/structure;
- reactive/coercive restriction dapat thwart autonomy dan menimbulkan concealment/reactance.

Karena literature DPM memang mixed [@vossen2024parenting; @ren2022mediation], direction restrictive DPM **tidak dikunci sebagai universal positive/negative hypothesis**.

### Observant mediation

Monitoring/inspection dapat dipersepsikan sebagai protective involvement atau intrusive surveillance. Tanpa evidence tentang transparency, trust, dan adolescent autonomy, direction terhadap SEC/engagement tidak dapat diturunkan secara aman.

**Decision:** primary theory-derived DPM path difokuskan pada enabling/interpretative dimension; restrictive dan observant dimensions dipertahankan sebagai distinct predictors dengan non-directional/conditional expectations sampai #22/#23 menentukan estimand final.

## 4. Cross-context logic

Neo-ecological framing membuat pertanyaan yang lebih substantif daripada dua parallel arrows: apakah family digital context dan teacher support **reinforce** atau **compensate** satu sama lain?

Longitudinal evidence menunjukkan different social support sources tidak identik: Wang & Eccles menemukan teacher, parent, dan peer support memiliki relative associations yang berbeda terhadap engagement trajectories dari middle ke high school [@wangeccles2012support]. Existing Jelas et al. menunjukkan parent/teacher/peer support dapat coexist dalam engagement model [@jelas2016learningsupport].

Dari sini dua competing cross-context predictions diperoleh:

1. **Reinforcement hypothesis:** enabling DPM memiliki association dengan SEC/engagement yang lebih kuat ketika PTS tinggi, karena coherent support across contexts facilitates adaptive functioning.
2. **Compensation hypothesis:** PTS memiliki association dengan SEC/engagement yang lebih kuat ketika enabling DPM rendah, karena supportive school context substitutes for weaker family digital guidance.

**Neither direction is assumed.** #22 harus mencari falsification/precedent dan menentukan apakah interaction/configuration claim cukup novel dan estimable.

## 5. Construct-to-theory map

| Construct | Theoretical role | What theory supports | What theory does NOT support |
|---|---|---|---|
| DPM | family-digital microsystem practice | context-specific socialization; possible need support/thwarting depending implementation | one global beneficial DPM factor |
| PTS | school/teacher support resource | need support; relational/instrumental resources; engagement mechanism | objective teacher competence |
| SEC | developmental capacity | plausible regulatory/social mechanism enabling adaptive participation | direct derivation from SDT |
| Engagement | adaptive school functioning outcome | SDT/self-system outcome of supported needs/motivation | generic “readiness” proxy |
| Need satisfaction/autonomous motivation | competing proximal mechanism | direct SDT mediator from support to engagement | SEC equivalent |
| SRL/self-efficacy | rival personal mechanisms | plausible alternative process | components of SEC by default |

## 6. Path audit

### PTS → Engagement

**RETAIN, theory-derived.** SDT/self-system logic plus meta-analytic and longitudinal evidence provide strong support [@tao2022teachersupport; @vargasmadriz2024support; @martin2024support]. Causal language remains restricted under observational design.

### PTS → SEC

**RETAIN as developmental/empirical pathway, not pure SDT path.** Supportive teaching is associated with selected emotion-regulation/conflict-resolution competencies [@collie2022supportsec], but full multidimensional SEC mechanism remains to be tested.

### SEC → Engagement

**RETAIN, theory-compatible developmental capacity pathway.** Direct systematic-review evidence supports association [@santos2023secengagement].

### PTS → SEC → Engagement

**RETAIN as primary candidate indirect pathway, but explicitly compete with SDT mediator.** This is the cleanest school-side mediation candidate after construct lock.

### Enabling DPM → SEC

**RETAIN with qualification.** DPM→self-/emotion-regulation evidence is construct-adjacent [@chen2016mediation], so full SEC indirect pathway is weaker than PTS pathway.

### Restrictive/observant DPM → SEC/Engagement

**DO NOT impose directional hypotheses.** Treat as strategy-specific exploratory/conditional paths unless #22 finds stronger theory-matched evidence.

### Global DPM → Engagement

**DROP as a single theory-derived path.** A total DPM score is already rejected by #17.

## 7. Competing mechanisms map

The minimum competing explanation set for #22/#23 is:

1. **Basic psychological need satisfaction / autonomous motivation** — strongest theory-derived rival for PTS→engagement under SDT.
2. **Academic self-efficacy** — teacher support can work through competence beliefs; also plausible for engagement.
3. **SRL/self-regulation** — especially plausible for DPM and engagement, but partially overlaps with SEC self-management.
4. **Teacher-student relationship** — relational quality may explain apparent PTS associations.
5. **Prior engagement** — engaged students can elicit more teacher/parent support; transactional/reverse path.
6. **General parenting/parent-child relationship** — can explain associations attributed to DPM.
7. **Peer support/classroom climate** — alternative school social ecology.

Not all variables should be entered simultaneously as controls. Some are mediators, exposures, colliders, or alternative models. #23 must define DAG/estimands first.

## 8. Theoretical boundary conditions

Predictions are expected to vary by:

- adolescence stage/grade because autonomy needs and adult support change developmentally;
- DPM implementation quality (collaborative/rationalized vs reactive/coercive);
- support domain (emotional, instrumental, informational, appraisal);
- engagement dimension (behavioral, emotional, cognitive, possibly agentic);
- teacher/class/school context and clustering;
- baseline student functioning because support is transactional rather than purely one-directional.

DKI is **not** declared a theoretical boundary condition unless #21/#22 identify a mechanism showing why the same process should differ specifically in DKI.

## 9. Revised theory-derived hypotheses

Working hypotheses for downstream falsification:

- **H1:** Higher perceived teacher support is associated with higher subsequent student engagement, conditional on baseline engagement and design covariates.
- **H2:** Higher perceived teacher support is associated with higher subsequent SEC, conditional on baseline SEC where measured.
- **H3:** Higher SEC is associated with higher subsequent engagement, conditional on baseline engagement.
- **H4:** The PTS–engagement association includes an indirect longitudinal component through SEC; this indirect pathway is compared with a theoretically stronger proximal SDT rival where feasible.
- **H5:** Enabling/interpretative DPM is positively associated with selected SEC domains; evidence is weaker than H1–H3 and the hypothesis remains qualification-sensitive.
- **H6:** Cross-context DPM×PTS configuration is evaluated as competing reinforcement versus compensation, not assigned a directional sign in advance.

Restrictive and observant DPM are estimated separately and interpreted strategy-specifically; no global beneficial hypothesis is imposed.

## 10. What Gemma does not test

- not a full test of Bronfenbrenner PPCT;
- not a causal test of SDT unless an intervention/exogenous design is introduced;
- not a test that all DPM is beneficial;
- not a test of objective teacher competence;
- not evidence that SEC is the sole mediator;
- not proof that DKI changes theoretical processes;
- not evidence of mesosystem effects merely because family and school predictors appear in one SEM.

A mesosystem/cross-context claim requires actual interaction/configuration or relational linkage between contexts.

## 11. Theoretical contribution candidate

The defensible candidate contribution is **not “applying Bronfenbrenner + SDT.”** Theory application by itself is not novelty.

A stronger candidate is:

> to determine whether multidimensional family-digital mediation and perceived teacher support show distinct or cross-context associations with adolescent engagement, and whether SEC adds explanatory value relative to a more proximal motivational mechanism predicted by SDT.

This contribution has three falsifiable components:

1. strategy-specific rather than global DPM;
2. cross-context reinforcement/compensation rather than parallel predictor stacking;
3. mechanism discrimination (SEC versus proximal motivational mechanism).

Whether these are genuinely novel remains an empirical literature question for #22.

## 13. Decision

**ISSUE #20 SUBSTANTIVE DECISION: PASS.**

The final working architecture is **neo-ecological positioning + SDT/self-system proximal engagement mechanism + SEC as a distinct developmental capacity mechanism tested against motivational rivals**. The strongest theory-derived chain is PTS→motivational resources/engagement; PTS→SEC→engagement is retained as a competing developmental pathway. DPM is strategy-specific: enabling mediation receives the strongest directional rationale, while restrictive and observant mediation remain conditional. Cross-context reinforcement versus compensation is elevated as a real ecological question for novelty falsification rather than claiming a mesosystem merely from parallel predictors.
