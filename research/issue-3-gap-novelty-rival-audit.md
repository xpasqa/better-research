# Issue #3 — Gap, Novelty, dan Rival-Explanation Audit Gemma

## 0. Audit rule

Research gap tidak ditentukan dari banyaknya variabel yang digabungkan, lokasi penelitian, mediator, atau pilihan software/statistical estimator. Gap harus berasal dari **unresolved explanatory, theoretical, measurement, identification, atau boundary-condition problem** yang benar-benar didukung evidence.

Audit ini menggunakan evidence base Issue #4 dan targeted falsification search tambahan. Search tambahan sengaja mencari model yang serupa—family/school support, student mediator, digital context, social-emotional competence, engagement/achievement—untuk melemahkan klaim novelty-of-combination.

Targeted search tidak setara dengan exhaustive systematic review seluruh database. Karena itu audit ini **tidak** menyimpulkan “belum pernah ada model yang identik”.

## 2. Falsification search: models that weaken the original novelty claim

Three verified adjacent studies are especially important.

### 2.1 Family + school + mediator + academic outcome already exists

Jelas et al. examined parent, teacher, and peer learning support, student engagement, and academic achievement among 2,359 Malaysian adolescents [@jelas2016learningsupport]. The constructs are not identical to Gemma, but the **configuration logic**—multiple social contexts -> student mechanism -> academic outcome—is not novel.

Therefore, the statement “novel because family and school variables are integrated through a mediator” is rejected.

### 2.2 School + digital context + mediation already exists

An et al. examined perceived teacher support, technology acceptance, learning motivation, and learning engagement among middle-school students [@an2022teachersupport]. This again is not the Gemma model, but it shows that school support, digital/technology processes, and mediated learning outcomes are already integrated in adolescent research.

Therefore, “digital context + school variable + mediation” is not novelty by itself.

### 2.3 SEC + teacher context + academic engagement already exists

Yin et al. examined social-emotional competence and teachers' caring behavior in a moderated mediation model of adolescent academic engagement [@yin2023secengagement]. This directly weakens the claim that introducing SEC/SEL as an intervening student mechanism alongside a teacher-context variable is inherently novel.

Therefore, “SEC as mediator with teacher variable” cannot be claimed as standalone novelty.

## 3. Research gap claims that are rejected

The following claims are **not defensible** from the current evidence:

1. “Belum pernah ada penelitian yang menggabungkan Pengasuhan Digital, Kompetensi Guru, SEL, dan Kesiapan Belajar.”
2. “Masih sedikit penelitian” without a reproducible database-wide search protocol and denominator.
3. “DKI Jakarta belum pernah diteliti” as a substantive research gap.
4. “Menggunakan SEM-PLS” as methodological novelty.
5. “Menggunakan SEL/SEC sebagai mediator” as novelty.
6. “Mengintegrasikan faktor keluarga dan sekolah” as novelty.
7. “Menggunakan empat variabel sekaligus” as novelty.
8. “Hasil penelitian sebelumnya tidak konsisten” as sufficient gap without identifying why inconsistency matters theoretically/methodologically.

## 4. Candidate gaps that survive audit

### 4.1 Construct/measurement problem — strongest immediate problem, but not yet a contribution

The strongest finding from Issue #4 is not a literature void; it is a **construct-validity problem** in the draft.

- Z is labeled SEL but measured as student competence domains.
- X2 may be objective teacher competence in wording but student-perceived competence/support in data.
- Y has no verified basis as a broad generic “learning readiness” construct for SMP.

This is a fundamental design problem that must be solved. Resolving one's own measurement ambiguity is **necessary for validity**, but it is not automatically a doctoral theoretical contribution.

### 4.2 Explanatory/mechanism gap — candidate, not established gap

Evidence supports separate links among parenting practices, teacher-related contexts, social-emotional/self-regulatory capacities, and learning outcomes, but the current audit does not establish a construct-matched explanation of how **digital family practices and student-experienced school conditions jointly relate to a clearly defined learning-functioning outcome through SEC**.

This is a plausible mechanism question. It becomes a defensible gap only after:

- X1, X2, Z, Y are construct-valid;
- direct path evidence is mapped more exhaustively;
- rival mediators (engagement, teacher-student relationship, classroom climate, motivation, self-efficacy) are considered;
- temporal/causal language is proportional to design.

### 4.3 Configuration gap — limited and incremental unless mechanism matters

The exact configuration of digital parenting + a student-perceived school construct + SEC + a validated learning outcome may be less commonly tested than adjacent configurations. However, **configuration novelty alone is weak**. It is useful only if the combination allows a theoretically meaningful comparison of family and school processes or tests a mechanism that existing models leave unresolved.

### 4.4 Level-of-analysis gap — potentially defensible as problem framing

Much public evidence about DKI concerns regional access, schooling, or institutional indicators. These data cannot explain individual students' readiness, engagement, or SEC. The research can legitimately move from **contextual conditions** to an individual-level explanatory question, provided it does not commit ecological inference.

This is more defensible as problem framing than as novelty claim.

### 4.5 Boundary-condition gap — not yet established

DKI's urban-digital setting could be theoretically relevant, particularly under a neo-ecological perspective [@navarro2023neoecological], but there is currently no evidence that DKI specifically changes the direction or magnitude of the hypothesized relationships.

Therefore, DKI should remain a **setting** unless the design includes a meaningful comparison/moderator or theory-supported contextual mechanism.

## 5. Rival explanations that must be recognized

### Family/student side

- socioeconomic status;
- parental education;
- general parental involvement/warmth/autonomy support outside digital parenting;
- student's prior achievement;
- baseline motivation/self-efficacy;
- digital literacy;
- type/purpose of digital use, not only screen duration;
- adolescent behavior influencing later parenting (reverse/child effect) [@tong2024internetparenting].

### School side

- teacher-student relationship [@emslander2025teacherstudent; @magro2023studentteacher];
- perceived teacher support [@tao2022teachersupport];
- classroom climate;
- school resources;
- peer support/influence;
- instructional quality distinct from teacher competence.

### Alternative student mechanisms

- engagement [@tao2022teachersupport; @jelas2016learningsupport];
- learning motivation [@an2022teachersupport];
- self-efficacy;
- self-regulation [@chen2016mediation];
- resilience;
- teacher/student relationship processes rather than SEC.

These rivals do not all have to become control variables. Selection of controls must follow theory/causal structure; indiscriminate statistical adjustment can introduce bias.

## 6. Novelty audit

### Novelty claims currently prohibited

- first study;
- only study;
- unique integration;
- DKI as novelty;
- PLS-SEM as novelty;
- mediation as novelty;
- CASEL five competencies as novelty.

### Novelty wording currently defensible

The strongest safe formulation is **potential contribution**, not established novelty:

> Penelitian ini berpotensi memperluas evidence mengenai bagaimana praktik pengasuhan dalam konteks digital dan kondisi sekolah yang dialami siswa berkaitan dengan fungsi belajar pada siswa SMP, dengan kompetensi sosial-emosional sebagai candidate intervening mechanism. Nilai kontribusi model bergantung pada penyelesaian batas konstruk, pemilihan outcome yang tervalidasi, pengukuran yang selaras dengan informan, dan pengujian terhadap rival explanations.

Qualification wajib:

- tidak menyatakan model belum pernah diuji;
- tidak menyatakan DKI menghasilkan kontribusi teori secara otomatis;
- tidak menyatakan indirect statistical effect sebagai causal mechanism;
- tidak menyatakan SEC mediator utama/unik tanpa comparison/rival evidence.

## 7. Contribution audit

### Theoretical contribution

**Belum established.** Dapat muncul bila model menghasilkan explanatory insight yang mengubah/menajamkan pemahaman hubungan family-school contexts dengan student functioning, bukan hanya menunjukkan path signifikan.

### Empirical contribution

**Plausible, conditional.** Evidence individual-level dari siswa SMP DKI dapat menambah basis empiris pada konteks urban Indonesia, tetapi ini terutama contextual extension kecuali menghasilkan boundary condition atau pattern yang theoretically informative.

### Methodological contribution

**Tidak established.** SEM/PLS, mediation, Likert scale, atau sample DKI bukan contribution. Measurement clarification dapat meningkatkan rigor tetapi bukan novelty otomatis.

### Practical/policy contribution

**Plausible setelah hasil.** Tidak boleh dijanjikan sebelum construct validity dan estimates tersedia.

## 8. Decision gate sebelum final Bab 1

1. **Y harus diselesaikan**: pertahankan hanya jika ada construct definition + validated instrument yang sesuai SMP/research purpose; jika tidak, re-specify outcome.
2. **Z harus diputuskan**: SEC jika lima CASEL competencies diukur sebagai student attributes; SEL jika process/exposure/implementation yang diukur.
3. **X2 harus selaras dengan informant**: objective teacher competence vs student-perceived teacher support/quality/behavior.
4. Gap harus ditulis sebagai unresolved explanation/measurement/configuration problem, bukan novelty-of-combination.
5. DKI diposisikan sebagai setting sampai ada basis boundary-condition yang lebih kuat.

## 9. Kesimpulan Issue #3

**Current novelty claim dari draft awal tidak lolos audit.** Literature yang diverifikasi menunjukkan bahwa integrasi family-school contexts, student mediators, digital processes, SEC, engagement, dan academic outcomes sudah hadir dalam berbagai konfigurasi berdekatan. Target kontribusi yang lebih defensible adalah explanatory clarification yang construct-valid dan rival-aware—bukan klaim “belum pernah menggabungkan”.

Issue #5 dapat melanjutkan rekonstruksi Bab 1 dengan dua syarat: (1) uncertainty Y/Z/X2 ditampilkan eksplisit; dan (2) gap/novelty ditulis proporsional sesuai audit ini.
