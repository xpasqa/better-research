## Language policy

Repository documentation is maintained in English for global portability. The research itself may use any language required by the institution, discipline, participants, sources, or intended audience.

For each major output, record:

- primary language;
- required translation(s), if any;
- terminology conventions;
- treatment of quotations in another language;
- whether translated quotations require the original text alongside the translation;
- which version is authoritative when multiple language versions exist.

Translation must not strengthen or weaken claims, remove uncertainty, alter technical meaning, or obscure attribution.
