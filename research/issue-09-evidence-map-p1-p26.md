## Unsupported / Weakly Supported Claims to Watch

1. Any claim that Korbrimob or Polri is experiencing **performance decline**.
2. Any statement that low certification percentage by itself means competence is “inadequate”.
3. Any generalization from Pasukan Gegana to all Korbrimob without qualification.
4. Any claim that digital infrastructure has already caused better personnel performance.
5. Any statement that Digital Leadership, Digital Workplace, Engagement, and Performance have “never” been studied together.
6. Any direct equation of Digital Leadership with a JD-R job resource.
7. Any definition of Digital Workplace as inherently positive if the eventual instrument only captures positively valenced features.
8. Any causal wording drawn only from cross-sectional SEM studies.
