# Issue #13 — Audit JD-R, Engagement Mechanism, and Construct Boundaries

## Executive Conclusion

JD-R **layak dipakai sebagai primary motivational lens** untuk menjelaskan jalur resources/conditions → work engagement → performance, tetapi tidak cukup jika dipakai secara mekanistik untuk membenarkan seluruh direct paths. Konstruksi model paling defensible adalah:

- **Digital Leadership (X1)** = upstream organizational/social **resource-shaping condition**;
- **Digital Workplace (X2)** = configuration/quality of digitally mediated work conditions; beberapa atribut yang positif dapat berfungsi sebagai resources, sedangkan broader digitalization juga dapat menimbulkan demands;
- **Work Engagement (Z)** = motivational state/process yang mencerminkan vigor, dedication, absorption;
- **Individual Work Performance (Y)** = individual-level work behavior/outcome, bukan unit KPI atau organizational performance.

## Construct Boundary Memo

### X1 — Kepemimpinan Digital / Digital Leadership

**Core boundary:**

Digital Leadership bukan sekadar kemampuan pimpinan menggunakan perangkat digital. Berdasarkan review Lin (2024), konstruk ini mencakup digital vision/strategic thinking, empowerment, innovation, cross-boundary collaboration, dynamic adaptation, digital change, dan talent development.

**What X1 is not:**

- digital competence personel;
- transformational leadership generik;
- organizational digital culture;
- digital workplace quality;
- technology adoption itself.

**JD-R positioning:**

Jangan tulis “Digital Leadership adalah job resource” sebagai definisi langsung. Posisi yang lebih tepat:

> Digital Leadership merupakan upstream organizational/social condition yang dapat membentuk atau memperkuat resources seperti clarity, support, empowerment, learning opportunities, coordination, access to information/resources, dan adaptation support.

Evidence longitudinal/meta-analytic JD-R menunjukkan leader-level dan organizational-level resources dapat memprediksi engagement, tetapi konstruk Digital Leadership sendiri tetap harus dibedakan dari resource yang dihasilkannya.

### X2 — Lingkungan Kerja Digital / Digital Workplace

**Core boundary:**

Digital Workplace adalah kondisi/konfigurasi lingkungan kerja yang semakin dimediasi teknologi dalam akses informasi, communication, coordination, collaboration, task execution, integration dan support.

**What X2 is not:**

- sekadar jumlah aplikasi/perangkat;
- digital transformation pada level organisasi secara keseluruhan;
- technostress;
- digital literacy/competence;
- remote work saja;
- digital job demands secara umum.

**Critical measurement warning:**

Literatur terbaru menunjukkan broader workplace digitalization bersifat double-edged. Zhan & Xie (2025) menemukan challenge/threat pathways; Xu et al. (2025) menunjukkan positive dan negative employee outcomes. Karena itu:

- jika instrumen X2 nanti **positively valenced**, definisi operasional harus fokus pada supportive/enabling digital-work conditions;
- technostress, overload, fragmentation dan threat appraisal diperlakukan sebagai boundary/rival conditions, bukan dicampur ke skor konstruk yang sama tanpa dasar measurement model.

### Z — Keterikatan Personel / Work Engagement

**Core boundary:**

Keterikatan Personel harus di-anchor sebagai **work engagement**, bukan employee engagement generik: keadaan positif terkait pekerjaan yang ditandai vigor, dedication, absorption.

**Distinguish from:**

- organizational commitment;
- job involvement;
- job satisfaction;
- affective commitment;
- techno-work engagement/digital engagement jika definisinya berbeda.

**Mechanism role:**

JD-R motivational process memberi alasan teoretis mengapa resources/conditions dapat meningkatkan investasi energi, persistence dan dedication yang kemudian berhubungan dengan task/contextual performance. Meta-analysis Mazzetti et al. mendukung resources→engagement; Corbeanu & Iliescu serta Neuber et al. mendukung engagement→performance.

Engagement dengan demikian **bukan mediator karena SEM memungkinkan indirect effect**, tetapi mediator karena ada motivational mechanism yang dapat dipertanggungjawabkan.

### Y — Kinerja Personel / Individual Work Performance

**Core boundary:**

Y harus konsisten pada **individual work performance**.

**Distinguish from:**

- organizational performance;
- unit KPI/LKIP;
- public-service quality;
- perceived system performance;
- innovative performance;
- sustainability performance.

Evidence dengan outcome lain boleh dipakai untuk contextual/theoretical triangulation, tetapi tidak boleh ditulis seolah direct evidence untuk konstruk Y yang identik.

## Theoretical Mechanism Map

### X1 → Z

Plausible mechanism:

`Digital Leadership → clarity/support/empowerment/learning/coordination → higher resource availability / stronger motivational investment → Work Engagement`

Evidence support:

- Li et al. (2024): empowerment + affective commitment mechanisms;
- Li, Wang & Han (2026): digital self-efficacy mediator, technostress moderator;
- broader JD-R evidence: leader-level resources predict engagement.

**Assessment:** STRONG PLAUSIBILITY, not causal certainty.

### X2 → Z

Plausible positive mechanism when X2 captures enabling conditions:

`Integrated/usability-supportive digital work → information/coordination/access resources → lower friction / greater goal attainment capacity → Work Engagement`

Competing negative pathway in broader digitalization:

`Fragmentation/overload/threat/technostress → energy depletion/anxiety → lower engagement`

**Assessment:** PLAUSIBLE BUT CONDITIONAL; measurement boundary is critical.

### Z → Y

Mechanism:

`Vigor + dedication + absorption → sustained effort, persistence, concentration, proactive/task investment → Individual Work Performance`

Meta-analytic support is comparatively strong.

**Assessment:** STRONGEST EMPIRICAL LINK IN MODEL, but most evidence remains associational.

### X1 → Y direct path

Possible rationales outside pure JD-R mediation:

- clearer digital direction and decision support;
- coordination and resource allocation;
- empowerment/innovation behavior;
- reduced ambiguity / faster problem solving.

Existing studies show creativity, innovative behavior, organizational culture and other mechanisms. Therefore, a direct path may be empirically testable, but **JD-R alone should not be used as complete justification**.

**Assessment:** NEEDS SEPARATE BAB-II JUSTIFICATION.

### X2 → Y direct path

Possible rationale:

- better information access;
- communication/coordination affordances;
- lower workflow friction;
- system usability/integration;
- decision support.

But technology can also create overload/fragmentation and stress.

**Assessment:** NEEDS SEPARATE BAB-II JUSTIFICATION AND CLEAR X2 MEASUREMENT.

## JD-R Fit Assessment

| Model Component | JD-R Fit | Recommended Position |
|---|---|---|
| X1 Digital Leadership | Moderate–strong as upstream condition | Resource-shaping organizational/social condition |
| X2 Digital Workplace | Moderate–strong if positively defined | Work-context configuration containing resource-like characteristics; broader digitalization may also create demands |
| Z Work Engagement | Strong | Core motivational process/state |
| Y Individual Work Performance | Strong as downstream outcome | Outcome associated with engagement/resource process |
| X1→Y direct | Partial | Needs extra mechanism/theory, do not force into JD-R |
| X2→Y direct | Partial | Needs affordance/work-design/coordination rationale beyond generic JD-R |

## Rival Mechanisms / Boundary Conditions

### High-priority rival mechanisms

1. **Digital self-efficacy / digital competence** — can explain why the same leadership/digital environment yields different outcomes.
2. **Psychological empowerment** — repeatedly appears between leadership and engagement/performance.
3. **Affective commitment / meaningfulness** — alternative motivational route.
4. **Job crafting / innovative behavior / creativity** — alternative performance mechanisms.
5. **Organizational culture** — already used in Polri-specific DL-performance research.

### High-priority boundary conditions

1. technostress / digital fatigue;
2. digital literacy;
3. usability/integration/interoperability;
4. discretion and sense of control in policing;
5. hierarchy and operational task intensity;
6. resource scarcity/personnel readiness;
7. level of analysis and performance measure.

These factors should inform limitation, robustness, qualitative follow-up or future-research discussion; they are **not automatically added to the locked model**.

## Safe Wording for Bab I

### Safe

- “Kepemimpinan Digital diposisikan sebagai kondisi organisasional yang berpotensi membentuk resources bagi personel melalui arah, dukungan, koordinasi, empowerment dan pembelajaran.”
- “Lingkungan Kerja Digital yang mendukung akses informasi, koordinasi dan penyelesaian tugas dapat menyediakan resource-like conditions, sementara literature digitalization juga menunjukkan adanya demands seperti technostress dan fragmentation.”
- “Keterikatan Personel diperlakukan sebagai motivational mechanism yang secara teoritis dapat menghubungkan conditions/resources dengan Kinerja Personel.”
- “Hubungan tersebut perlu diuji dalam konteks Korbrimob; evidence terdahulu tidak dianggap membuktikan causal effect pada setting ini.”

### Unsafe / Avoid

- “Digital Leadership adalah job resource.”
- “Digital Workplace selalu meningkatkan engagement.”
- “Engagement menyebabkan performance meningkat” tanpa design qualification.
- “JD-R membuktikan seluruh direct dan indirect paths.”
- “Technostress adalah dimensi negatif Digital Workplace” tanpa dukungan measurement model.
