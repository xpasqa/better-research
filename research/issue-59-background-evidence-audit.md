# Issue #59 — Evidence, gap, dan quality audit untuk 1.1 Latar Belakang

## Keputusan

**PASS UNTUK DRAFT PROPOSAL, DENGAN BATAS KLAIM EKSPLISIT.**

Evidence yang tersedia memadai untuk merekonstruksi 1.1 Latar Belakang secara problem-driven. Tidak ditemukan masalah fundamental yang mengharuskan konstruk utama diganti kembali. Namun, kekuatan evidence antarjalur tidak simetris: hubungan Dukungan Guru–Keterlibatan Belajar relatif lebih kuat; hubungan Mediasi Digital Orang Tua dengan outcome belajar sekolah masih lebih tidak langsung dan heterogen. DKI Jakarta harus tetap diposisikan sebagai konteks empiris dan batas transferabilitas, bukan sebagai bukti krisis engagement atau novelty teoritis.

## Tahap 1 — Diagnosis ringkas

- **Fenomena utama:** pengalaman belajar remaja berlangsung dalam ekologi yang menghubungkan sekolah, keluarga, dan lingkungan digital.
- **Outcome problem:** Keterlibatan Belajar penting dan dinamis pada masa remaja, tetapi tidak ada evidence yang cukup untuk menyatakan bahwa siswa SMP DKI secara umum mengalami krisis engagement.
- **Empirical tension:** teacher support–engagement relatif konsisten tetapi heterogen; digital parental mediation memiliki bentuk strategi yang berbeda dan hasil yang mixed.
- **Explanatory puzzle:** apakah konteks keluarga digital dan dukungan guru berkaitan dengan engagement melalui proses yang sama atau berbeda.
- **Mekanisme:** SEC = kemampuan siswa; BPNS = pengalaman terpenuhinya kebutuhan otonomi, kompetensi, dan keterhubungan di sekolah. Keduanya tidak digabung.
- **Teori:** Bioekologi/Neo-ekologi untuk penempatan konteks; SDT khusus menjelaskan PTS → BPNS → Engagement.
- **Level analisis:** individu siswa; data agregat DKI hanya contextual evidence.
- **Inferensi:** desain longitudinal tiga gelombang memperbaiki urutan waktu, tetapi tetap observasional dan tidak mengidentifikasi causal effect.

### Falsification findings

1. **DPM is not uniformly beneficial.** Huang et al. and Vossen et al. directly undermine a simple `more mediation = better outcome` narrative.
2. **PTS is not universally dominant.** Meta-analytic average association is positive, but heterogeneity and null/conditional evidence (e.g., Ansong et al.) remain.
3. **SEC is not the only plausible mechanism.** BPNS, self-regulation, self-efficacy, emotion regulation, and motivation are credible alternatives. The proposal therefore retains BPNS as a theory-derived comparator but does not add a kitchen-sink set of mediators.
4. **Temporal direction SEC↔engagement is not established.** Reverse/reciprocal patterns exist, so longitudinal ordering is useful but not causal proof.
5. **DKI is not novelty by itself.** Current official evidence supports contextual description, not a theoretical boundary effect.

## Tahap 4 — Gap audit

### Relatively established

- Engagement is multidimensional and developmentally dynamic during adolescence.
- Teacher support is positively associated with engagement on average.
- SEC is generally positively associated with engagement.
- SDT provides a coherent explanation for supportive teaching → psychological need satisfaction → adaptive learning functioning.

### Mixed/conditional

- Which forms of DPM are helpful, neutral, or counterproductive.
- Strength of teacher support across support domains, engagement dimensions, designs, and contexts.
- Temporal ordering between SEC and engagement.
- Whether SEC retains explanatory value when a more proximal SDT mechanism such as BPNS is considered.

### Alternative explanations

- reverse child effects on parental mediation;
- prior engagement shaping perceived support;
- self-regulation/self-efficacy/motivation as alternative mechanisms;
- family climate/general parenting rather than internet-specific mediation;
- measurement/informant effects;
- class/school clustering and contextual variation.

### Defensible gap

**Explanatory/temporal gap:** evidence has identified relevant family, school, and student factors, but it remains useful to test how strategy-specific digital parental mediation and perceived teacher support relate prospectively to engagement, and whether the pathways are better represented through student social-emotional competence, school-based basic psychological need satisfaction, or different pathways for family and school contexts.

### Claims yang tidak boleh dibuat

- “Siswa SMP DKI mengalami krisis keterlibatan belajar.”
- “Belum pernah ada penelitian yang menggabungkan variabel-variabel ini.”
- “DPM selalu meningkatkan engagement.”
- “Teacher support pasti menyebabkan engagement.”
- “SEC terbukti sebagai mediator kausal.”
- “DKI adalah theoretical boundary condition.”
- “Longitudinal tiga gelombang membuktikan kausalitas.”
