## Level-of-Analysis Memo

### What the LKIP can support

LKIP Pasukan Gegana dapat digunakan untuk menunjukkan bahwa pada **unit Pasukan Gegana** terdapat kombinasi:

- formal target achievement/exceedance;
- personnel-strength constraints relative to DSP;
- continuing training/development/certification activity;
- operational deployment demands;
- stated need for professional capability development.

### What the LKIP cannot support

LKIP tersebut **tidak** dapat digunakan untuk menyimpulkan:

- individual work performance personel Korbrimob secara langsung;
- seluruh Korbrimob memiliki angka SDM yang sama dengan Pasukan Gegana;
- engagement personel rendah/tinggi;
- digital leadership rendah/tinggi;
- digital workplace buruk/baik;
- digitalization caused performance improvement/decline.

### Safe empirical tension

Safe formulation:

> Evidence Pasukan Gegana menunjukkan bahwa sejumlah target formal organisasi dapat dicapai atau dilampaui pada saat unit tetap menghadapi gap kekuatan personel relatif terhadap DSP dan kebutuhan pengembangan profesional/kemampuan. Kondisi ini mendukung formulasi **performance-sustainability/readiness/capability tension**, bukan klaim penurunan kinerja.

## Claim Decisions

### Keep

- Korbrimob mandate/high-intensity task context.
- NCSA/digital infrastructure and modernization direction.
- system integration/vendor/SDM TIK/continuity challenges.
- 42-person AI/database training as capability-development evidence.
- LKIP formal target achievements.
- DSP vs actual personnel **only for Pasukan Gegana**.

### Qualify

- “Korbrimob menghadapi kekurangan personel” → safer: “LKIP Pasukan Gegana mencatat…” unless all-Korbrimob source is obtained.
- “Digitalisasi meningkatkan kinerja” → replace with “ditujukan/diharapkan mendukung…” unless causal study is cited.
- “Sertifikasi masih rendah” → replace with exact coverage and target status; no evaluative label without benchmark.

### Remove/Avoid

- “semi-military” as an institutional fact.
- “performance decline” or “low personnel performance” without direct evidence.
- generalized personnel statistics from Gegana to all Korbrimob.
- statements that technology availability proves individual performance gains.
