# Issue #17 — Systematic Research Gap Audit and Literature Synthesis Bab I–II

## Epistemic Status

Audit ini menggunakan verified literature matrix Issue #16, source-quality notes, serta targeted falsification search melalui Consensus Pro. Audit ini **bukan klaim bahwa seluruh literatur global telah dicakup secara exhaustif seperti systematic review PRISMA**. Tujuannya lebih sempit: menguji apakah gap yang hendak dipakai pada proposal masih bertahan ketika dihadapkan pada literature yang paling relevan, termasuk counter-evidence.

Kesimpulan karena itu harus dibaca sebagai **defensible research-gap formulation for the current dissertation proposal**, bukan universal no-study claim.

## Executive Conclusion

Research gap masih dapat dipertahankan, tetapi **bukan** dalam bentuk:

- “Digital Leadership belum diteliti terhadap engagement/performance”;
- “Digital Workplace belum diteliti terhadap engagement/performance”;
- “belum ada penelitian yang menggabungkan Digital Leadership dan Digital Workplace”;
- “policing/Polri belum pernah diteliti”;
- “engagement–performance masih inkonsisten” sebagai standalone gap;
- “SEM-PLS/mediation/Korbrimob” sebagai novelty.

Berdasarkan evidence yang terverifikasi, gap paling defensible adalah kombinasi **mechanism/configuration gap + boundary-condition gap + level-of-analysis qualification**. Intinya bukan absennya hubungan antarvariabel, melainkan belum mapannya penjelasan mengenai bagaimana dua kondisi digital-work yang secara konseptual berbeda—leadership-related dan workplace-related—diterjemahkan ke **individual work performance** melalui work engagement, terutama ketika digitalization sendiri dapat bersifat enabling maupun demanding dan ketika mekanisme tersebut diuji pada operational policing.

## Literature Synthesis Memo

### 1. Digital Leadership — apa yang sudah diketahui

Literatur Digital Leadership sudah berkembang dan tidak dapat lagi diperlakukan sebagai konstruk baru yang hampir tidak diteliti. Lin (2024) memetakan literature pada level organisasi, tim, dan individu. Empirical studies menunjukkan bahwa Digital Leadership berkaitan dengan Work Engagement (Li et al., 2024; Li, Wang, & Han, 2026) serta employee/job performance melalui beberapa mekanisme, termasuk creativity, innovative behavior, digital self-efficacy, empowerment, affective commitment, dan organizational culture.

**Consensus:** Digital Leadership secara umum berkaitan dengan outcome positif, tetapi mekanisme tidak tunggal.

**Contradiction/qualification:** hasil direct effect tidak harus selalu identik; sejumlah studi menunjukkan full/partial mediation atau conditional effects. Sebagian besar desain tetap observational.

**Remaining puzzle:** bukan “apakah DL berhubungan dengan outcome”, melainkan mekanisme mana yang relevan, bagaimana DL beroperasi bersama kondisi digital-work lain, dan apakah engagement memberi explanatory value tambahan dalam konfigurasi tertentu.

### 2. Digital Workplace / workplace digitalization — apa yang sudah diketahui

Evidence Digital Workplace/digitalization bersifat lebih heterogen. Chatterjee et al. (2023) menunjukkan bahwa Digital Workplace dan Digital Leadership capability telah ditempatkan dalam satu model terkait organizational performance. Selimović et al. (2021) menunjukkan pentingnya interpersonal/employee conditions dalam digital workplace transformation. Zhan dan Xie (2025) menunjukkan pada public-sector employees bahwa workplace digitalization dapat menghasilkan **challenge** maupun **threat appraisal**, sehingga engagement dapat bergerak melalui jalur positif maupun negatif. Meta-analysis Xu et al. (2025) memperkuat pola double-edged tersebut.

**Consensus:** teknologi/digitalization dapat menyediakan enabling conditions, tetapi tidak otomatis menjadi resource yang seragam.

**Contradiction/qualification:** dampak tergantung technology type, work configuration, appraisal, literacy, technostress, industry, dan demografi.

**Remaining puzzle:** jika X2 dioperasionalkan sebagai positively valenced Digital Workplace, perlu diuji apakah kualitas lingkungan digital tersebut berhubungan dengan engagement/performance setelah dibedakan dari broader digital demands.

### 3. Work Engagement dan JD-R — apa yang sudah diketahui

JD-R mempunyai basis teori dan evidence yang kuat untuk motivational process. Bakker dan Demerouti (2007, 2017) menyediakan teori dasar; Mazzetti et al. menunjukkan bahwa berbagai resources berkaitan dengan engagement; Lesener et al. memperlihatkan leader-, group-, dan organizational-level resources dapat memprediksi engagement secara longitudinal. Hubungan engagement–performance sendiri relatif kuat dibanding hubungan digital-specific lainnya: Corbeanu dan Iliescu serta Neuber et al. menyediakan meta-analytic evidence positif.

**Consensus:** Work Engagement layak diposisikan sebagai motivational mechanism yang plausible.

**Qualification:** engagement bukan satu-satunya mediator; reciprocal processes dan common-method issues tetap mungkin. JD-R juga tidak otomatis membenarkan setiap direct path.

**Remaining puzzle:** apakah engagement benar-benar menjadi mekanisme yang substantif ketika dua digital-work conditions diuji bersama dan performance diukur pada level individual yang konsisten.

### 4. Individual Work Performance — level outcome penting

Koopmans et al. (2014) dan evidence validasi Indonesia menguatkan bahwa Individual Work Performance merupakan construct tersendiri. Ini penting karena banyak literature digital transformation memakai organizational performance, innovation performance, sustainability performance, system performance, atau public-service outcome.

**Consensus:** outcome-level distinction bukan kosmetik.

**Remaining puzzle:** evidence organizational-performance tidak menjawab secara langsung mekanisme individual work performance. Demikian pula LKIP unit tidak dapat menggantikan pengukuran Y individual.

### 5. Integrated digital-work models — counter-evidence terhadap configuration gap yang terlalu luas

Claim “belum ada model yang menggabungkan kondisi digital leadership dan digital workplace/resources” tidak bertahan terhadap falsification.

- Chatterjee et al. (2023) secara eksplisit menggabungkan Digital Workplace dan Digital Leadership capability dalam menjelaskan organizational performance.
- Zia et al. (2024) menghubungkan digital job resources, digital engagement, digital leadership, dan innovative work behavior dalam serial mediation model, walaupun arah konstruk, definition, dan outcome berbeda dari model disertasi.
- Qiao, Li, dan Hong (2024) menguji Digital Leadership → Digital Transformation → employee performance/organizational commitment.

Ini berarti **configuration gap hanya defensible jika dirumuskan sempit**: current evidence tidak cukup untuk menyatakan bahwa *specific configuration* X1 Digital Leadership + X2 positively defined Digital Workplace → Z Work Engagement → Y Individual Work Performance sudah established atau universally transferable.

### 6. Public sector, policing, dan Polri — context bukan blank slate

Mergel et al. menunjukkan DT sektor publik mempunyai institutional constraints dan expected outcomes yang khas. Afzal dan Panagiotopoulos (2024) menunjukkan policing digital transformation melibatkan user adaptation, discretion, dan control. Henderikx dan Stoffers (2023) menunjukkan leadership behavior dalam digital transformation di police organization juga sudah diteliti. Silalahi et al. (2023) menyediakan Polri-specific evidence mengenai Digital Leadership dan performance melalui organizational culture, walaupun kualitas publikasi perlu diberi bobot lebih rendah.

**Consensus:** policing/Polri bukan konteks yang kosong dari penelitian.

**Remaining puzzle:** apakah explanatory configuration yang dikembangkan dari general/commercial/public-sector settings berlaku dengan pola yang sama pada operational policing di mana hierarchy, discretion, coordination, readiness, high-intensity task demands, dan public-security accountability berinteraksi dengan digital work.

## Falsification Tests

### Proposed claim A — “Belum ada studi yang mengintegrasikan leadership dan digital workplace.”

**Falsified.** Chatterjee et al. combines Digital Workplace and Digital Leadership capability; Zia et al. integrates digital job resources, engagement, and Digital Leadership. Therefore any absolute statement must be removed.

### Proposed claim B — “Work Engagement merupakan mediator yang belum digunakan pada Digital Leadership.”

**Falsified.** Li et al. directly study Digital Leadership–Work Engagement, while additional leadership/digital studies use engagement in mediation structures. The dissertation must justify *why engagement is theoretically appropriate here*, not claim it is unprecedented.

### Proposed claim C — “Digital Leadership pada Polri belum diteliti.”

**Falsified.** Silalahi et al. provides Polri-specific Digital Leadership/performance research; quality limitations do not erase existence.

### Proposed claim D — “Policing digital transformation belum memiliki human-side research.”

**Falsified.** Afzal & Panagiotopoulos and Henderikx & Stoffers directly address user adaptation/leadership in policing.

### Proposed claim E — “Engagement-performance relationship belum jelas.”

**Mostly falsified.** Large meta-analyses show a positive association. Remaining uncertainty concerns magnitude, causal direction, context, measurement, and boundary conditions—not the basic existence of association.

### Proposed claim F — “Specific X1+X2→Z→Y configuration pada operational policing sudah established.”

**Not supported by current verified evidence.** Related pieces and integrated neighboring models exist, but no fetched evidence in the current audit establishes this exact construct configuration, at the individual-performance level, within operational policing. Importantly, this is **not** equivalent to a universal claim that no such paper exists anywhere.

## Rival Explanations Audit

### Digital self-efficacy / competence
Li, Wang, dan Han menunjukkan digital self-efficacy sebagai mediator; Indonesian and public-sector digitalization studies also emphasize competence. This may explain heterogeneity in X1/X2 effects.

### Technostress / digital fatigue
High-priority boundary condition. It can weaken otherwise supportive digital conditions and makes uniformly positive X2 theorization untenable.

### Empowerment / affective commitment / meaningfulness
Li et al. shows empowerment and commitment mechanisms; leadership meta-analysis also identifies psychological needs/resources. These compete with a single-mediator engagement account.

### Innovative behavior / creativity / job crafting
Several DL-performance studies use these as performance translation mechanisms. Engagement is therefore one theoretically grounded mechanism among several.

### Organizational culture
Polri-specific Silalahi et al. uses culture as mediator. This matters in a hierarchical institutional context even if culture is not added to the locked model.

### System usability/integration
Official Korbrimob evidence identifies integration/vendor/SDM TIK problems. At individual level, system quality could confound perceptions of Digital Workplace and performance.

### User adaptation / discretion / control
Policing evidence indicates these are especially relevant. They help explain why operational-policing boundary conditions are theoretically meaningful rather than merely geographic.

## Final Research Gap Statement

### Gap 1 — Explanatory / Configuration Gap

Existing research has established multiple relationships among Digital Leadership, digitally mediated work conditions, Work Engagement, and performance, but the evidence remains distributed across different construct definitions, levels of analysis, outcomes, theories, and mediating mechanisms. Consequently, current evidence does not yet establish how **leadership-related digital conditions** and **workplace-related digital conditions** jointly translate into **Individual Work Performance** through **Work Engagement** within a single, level-consistent explanatory configuration. This gap concerns explanatory integration, not the novelty of combining variables.

### Gap 2 — Boundary-Condition / Transferability Gap

The applicability of that integrated motivational configuration to **operational policing** remains uncertain. Policing studies show that digital transformation is shaped by adaptation, discretion, control, hierarchy, coordination, and implementation conditions, while public-sector evidence also demonstrates sectoral differences in engagement and digital-work outcomes. Thus, it cannot be assumed that relationships observed in commercial, hybrid, or general public-sector samples transfer unchanged to a high-intensity policing environment such as Korbrimob.

### Gap 3 — Level-of-Analysis Qualification

A substantial portion of digital-transformation and Digital Workplace literature uses organizational, innovation, sustainability, or system-performance outcomes. Such evidence does not resolve the individual-level mechanism targeted in this dissertation. Likewise, Korbrimob/Gegana unit-level administrative indicators contextualize readiness and capability pressures but do not measure Individual Work Performance. This level distinction strengthens the rationale for an individual-level test but should be treated as a **design/measurement qualification**, not as standalone novelty.

## Contribution Calibration

### Potential Theoretical / Explanatory Contribution

The study can **test** whether an integrated configuration of Digital Leadership as a resource-shaping condition and a supportive Digital Workplace relates to Individual Work Performance directly and indirectly through Work Engagement. Contribution language must remain “test/extend/assess applicability,” not “introduce the first model.”

### Potential Empirical Contribution

Provide individual-level evidence on the human side of digital transformation in Korbrimob, while maintaining strict separation from unit-level KPI evidence.

### Potential Boundary / Context Contribution

Assess whether a motivational configuration supported mostly in commercial/general settings remains applicable under operational-policing conditions. The contribution lies in a theoretically meaningful boundary test, not location novelty.

### Potential Practical Contribution

Clarify whether leadership-related digital practices, enabling digital-work conditions, and work engagement appear to be actionable personnel-management levers associated with performance. Practical claims must follow effect sizes/robustness when data are available.

### Methodological Contribution

**None claimed at this stage.** SEM-PLS, mediation testing, or a mixed-method sequence are methods, not novelty by themselves.
