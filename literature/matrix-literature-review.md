## Synthesis status after Issue #16 outcome tournament

- **Y decision:** generic `Kesiapan Belajar Siswa` is **DROP**. Verified readiness evidence remains purpose-specific (online learning or school transition) and does not justify the draft mixture of motivation, discipline, adaptation, engagement and learning strategy.
- **Final working Y for downstream issues:** **Student Engagement in Learning / Keterlibatan Siswa dalam Pembelajaran**. It has strong adolescent measurement evidence, cross-national validation, Indonesian adaptation evidence, substantive links to achievement/conduct/dropout/graduation, and direct systematic-review evidence connecting SEC with engagement.
- **Competing outcome/mechanism:** Self-Regulated Learning is theoretically mature but overlaps strongly with SEC self-management/emotion regulation and motivation/self-efficacy; retain it as a rival mechanism rather than primary Y. School/Academic Adjustment remains a secondary broad candidate, not the primary outcome.
- **Unresolved measurement choice:** final engagement dimensionality (three vs four dimensions) and exact instrument are not yet locked; Indonesian invariance for DKI, school type and gender must be tested rather than assumed.
- **Novelty implication:** selecting engagement improves construct validity but does **not** create novelty. Final novelty must come from theory-driven mechanism discrimination, cross-context configuration or genuine boundary conditions.
