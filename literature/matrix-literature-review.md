## Synthesis status after Issues #16–#18 construct locks

- **Y:** generic readiness is dropped. Working Y is **Student Engagement in Learning**; final instrument/dimensionality awaits target-sample validation.
- **X1:** **Digital Parental Mediation (DPM)**, primarily adolescent-perceived and multidimensional; no global beneficial score.
- **X2:** `Kompetensi Guru` is dropped as primary X2. Working X2 is **Perceived Teacher Support (PTS) / Dukungan Guru yang Dipersepsikan Siswa**, with instrumental, emotional, informational and appraisal support as the primary measurement architecture candidate.
- **Level/informant:** PTS is an individual/dyadic student-perceived exposure, but ratings can contain shared teacher/class variance. IDs for teacher/class/school and ICC/multilevel diagnostics are mandatory in method design.
- **Rivals:** teacher professional competence, instructional quality, and teacher-student relationship are empirically important but conceptually distinct. They are not folded into PTS.
- **X2–Y evidence:** strong meta-analytic and longitudinal support for association/temporal plausibility, but observational evidence does not identify a causal effect.
- **X2–Z evidence:** selected secondary-school evidence supports a plausible relation between supportive teaching and social-emotional competencies; full SEC pathway remains unconfirmed pending #19–#20.
- **Novelty:** construct cleanup is validity improvement, not novelty. Final contribution still requires mechanism/configuration falsification.
