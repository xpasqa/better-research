## Synthesis status after Issue #22 novelty falsification

- **Rejected novelty:** X1-X2-Z-Y combination, DPM+SEC combination, SEC-as-mediator, DKI location, PTS→engagement, and any SEM/PLS-SEM claim.
- **Candidate explanatory contribution:** mechanism discrimination—whether family-digital and teacher-support contexts reach engagement through the same or different proximal mechanisms.
- **Primary competing mechanisms:** SEC versus SDT/basic-need satisfaction/autonomous motivation and self-regulation/self-efficacy. SEC cannot be privileged a priori.
- **Competing temporal direction:** engagement may precede later SEC/support processes; baseline engagement and temporal ordering are required.
- **Cross-context contribution:** reinforcement versus compensation between enabling DPM and PTS remains defensible only if explicitly modeled and adequately powered.
- **Empirical contribution:** individual-level SMP DKI evidence is a contextual/empirical extension whose value depends on probability-oriented sampling, construct validation, clustering treatment, and temporal design; it is not novelty by geography.
- **DPM implication:** enabling, restrictive and observant strategies remain distinct; no global positive DPM path is defensible.
- **PTS implication:** average association with engagement is supported, but null/weak/context-dependent results mean no universal direct-effect claim.
- **Contribution status:** theoretical contribution remains **candidate**, not established. It will only survive #23 if the design can compare mechanisms/configurations without becoming a kitchen-sink SEM.
