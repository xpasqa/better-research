## Synthesis status after Issues #16–#19 construct locks

- **X1:** multidimensional adolescent-perceived Digital Parental Mediation; strategy-specific and non-causal by default.
- **X2:** multidimensional Perceived Teacher Support; student-perceived/dyadic with possible shared class/teacher variance.
- **Z:** **Student Social-Emotional Competence**, not SEL process. Primary measurement candidate is Zhou & Ee SECQ; default is five correlated first-order factors. One higher-order global SEC factor is optional and must be tested against alternatives.
- **Y:** multidimensional Student Engagement in Learning.
- **Z boundary:** SEC is distinct from engagement, SRL, academic motivation/self-efficacy, and emotion regulation alone. Correlations require discriminant validity tests, not construct merging.
- **Antecedent evidence:** PTS→selected SEC domains is plausible; DPM→full SEC remains weak/construct-adjacent. Neither justifies a blanket causal effect.
- **Outcome evidence:** SEC–engagement has strong systematic-review support as association. Causal mediation is not established.
- **Mechanism implication:** SEC is retained as a primary candidate mediator but must compete conceptually/empirically with proximal mechanisms such as academic motivation/basic needs, self-efficacy, or SRL where theory warrants.
- **Method implication:** all four core constructs are multidimensional. Measurement model comparison, discriminant validity, clustering, temporal separation, and common-method mitigation are mandatory in #23.
