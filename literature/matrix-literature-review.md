## Synthesis status after Issues #4 and #3 targeted falsification search

- **Established with relatively strong evidence:** digital parenting is multidimensional and measurement-fragmented; teacher characteristics/competencies and teacher-student relations are associated with student outcomes; universal school-based SEL has broad positive effects; CASEL distinguishes the SEL process from competence domains; family/school/personal/peer factors jointly matter for secondary-school outcomes.
- **Mixed/conditional:** media-specific parenting effects; directionality of parent–adolescent digital behavior; conditions/moderators of SEL; teacher-side pathways and which construct best represents the school mechanism.
- **Already represented in adjacent integrated models:** family and school supports with student mediators and academic outcomes; teacher support with technology/motivation pathways to engagement; SEC combined with teacher caring in adolescent engagement models. Therefore, “integrating family + school + mediator + learning outcome” is not a defensible novelty claim.
- **Unknown/unresolved for Gemma:** the exact construct-matched X1→Z, X2→Z, Z→Y, X1→Y and X2→Y paths; validity of the draft's broad “Kesiapan Belajar” construct for SMP; whether SEC is the most appropriate mediator; whether DKI is a theoretical boundary condition rather than only a setting. A targeted search cannot justify the absolute claim that no identical model exists.
