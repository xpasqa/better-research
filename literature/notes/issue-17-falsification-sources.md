# Issue #17 — Targeted Falsification Sources

These sources were added after Issue #16 specifically to test whether a broad configuration-gap claim could survive counter-evidence. They are **not automatically manuscript citations**; they are audit evidence.

## Zia et al. (2024)

- **Title:** Digital job resources, digital engagement, digital leadership, and innovative work behaviour: a serial mediation model
- **Authors:** Ayesha Zia; M. Memon; Muhammad Zeeshan Mirza; Y. Iqbal; Adeel Tariq
- **Journal:** European Journal of Innovation Management
- **Year:** 2024
- **DOI:** 10.1108/EJIM-04-2023-0311
- **Design:** Cross-sectional, N=307 technological professionals, PLS-SEM
- **Why it matters:** Direct counter-evidence to any broad claim that digital resources, engagement, and Digital Leadership have never been integrated in one model. The direction/order and outcome differ from the Dodo model: digital job resources → digital engagement → digital leadership → innovative work behaviour.
- **Use:** Falsification only; does not establish the Dodo configuration.

## Qiao, Li, & Hong (2024)

- **Title:** The Strategic Role of Digital Transformation: Leveraging Digital Leadership to Enhance Employee Performance and Organizational Commitment in the Digital Era
- **Authors:** Guanming Qiao; Yannan Li; Ahreum Hong
- **Journal:** Systems
- **Year:** 2024
- **Volume/Page:** 12, 457
- **DOI:** 10.3390/systems12110457
- **Design:** Cross-sectional, N=579 across industries
- **Why it matters:** Shows an employee-performance pathway in which Digital Transformation mediates Digital Leadership, further weakening any claim that leadership→digital condition→employee-performance configurations are absent.
- **Use:** Falsification/competing mechanism; not direct evidence for Digital Workplace→Work Engagement→IWP.

## Audit Implication

These records strengthen a narrower gap formulation: the contribution cannot be the mere presence of Digital Leadership, digital-work resources/conditions, engagement-like constructs, and performance in one model. The defensible puzzle is the **specific level-consistent mechanism and its boundary applicability to operational policing**.
