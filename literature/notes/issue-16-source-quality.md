# Issue #16 — Source Quality and Exclusion Notes

## Purpose
Dokumen ini mencatat quality controls yang tidak boleh hilang ketika literature matrix digunakan untuk Bab I, Bab II, atau research-gap audit.

## High-Value Evidence Classes

1. **Foundational theory/review:** Vial (2019); Bakker & Demerouti (2007, 2017); Lin (2024).
2. **Meta-analytic evidence:** Decuypere & Schaufeli (2021); Mazzetti et al.; Corbeanu & Iliescu (2023); Neuber et al. (2021); Borst et al. (2020); Xu et al. (2025).
3. **Temporal/stronger observational evidence:** Lesener et al. (2020) longitudinal meta-analysis; Li, Wang, & Han (2026) two-wave; Zhan & Xie (2025) three-wave.
4. **Direct policing/public-sector relevance:** Afzal & Panagiotopoulos (2024); Henderikx & Stoffers (2023); Mergel et al. (2019).
5. **Measurement anchors:** Schaufeli, Bakker, & Salanova (2006); Koopmans et al. (2014); Widyastuti et al. (2024).

## Sources Requiring Explicit Qualification

### Chatterjee et al. (2023)
High relevance because it combines Digital Workplace and Digital Leadership capability, but the outcome is **organizational performance**, not Individual Work Performance. Use as configuration/level-of-analysis evidence, not a direct test of the Dodo model.

### Öngel et al.; Sağbaş et al.
Useful direct employee-performance evidence, but both are observational/cross-sectional. Their mediation paths support plausibility, not causal identification.

### Silalahi et al. (2023)
Relevant because it is Polri-specific and uses Digital Leadership, organizational culture, and performance. However, publication quality, construct operationalization, and cross-sectional design require caution. Do not give this paper the same evidentiary weight as meta-analyses or strong international journals merely because it has a DOI.

## Context Evidence Rule

Perpres, Polri/Humas Polri publications, NCSA institutional material, and LKIP Pasukan Gegana establish institutional facts and empirical setting. They do **not** establish causal relationships among Digital Leadership, Digital Workplace, Work Engagement, and Individual Work Performance.

## Causal-Inference Rule

Most construct-relation literature is observational. The matrix therefore separates:

- correlation/association;
- time-lagged ordering;
- mediation estimated from observational SEM;
- meta-analytic association;
- theoretical mechanism.

No relationship should be described as a causal effect solely because a path coefficient or indirect effect is statistically significant.

## Research-Gap Rule

Future-research statements from any single article are not research gaps by themselves. Gap candidates must survive cross-cluster synthesis and falsification in Issue #17.
